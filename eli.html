🔥 Waste Disposal and Incineration

For non-recyclable and hazardous materials that cannot be processed through conventional methods, Casper Waste Solutions operates state-of-the-art incineration facilities. Our high-temperature waste disposal systems safely destroy medical waste, chemical by-products, and other dangerous materials while capturing emissions to protect air quality. Incineration reduces waste volume by up to 90% and generates energy that powers our facilities, making it an efficient component of our integrated waste management strategy.

About Us

Casper Waste Solutions Limited is committed to creating a cleaner, healthier environment for everyone. We believe that proper waste management is not just a service — it is a responsibility toward our planet and future generations. Through innovative recycling programs, efficient collection systems, and community education, we are transforming how waste is handled in Karatina and beyond.

🌍 Our Mission

To provide sustainable waste management services that protect the environment, promote public health, and create value from waste through recycling and resource recovery.

♻️ What We Do

We collect waste products from residential areas, commercial establishments, and industrial sites. Our team sorts recyclable materials, processes organic waste into compost, and ensures hazardous materials are disposed of safely. By diverting waste from landfills, we help communities live in clean and friendly environments.

🌿 Environmental Impact

Every ton of waste we recycle saves energy, reduces pollution, and conserves natural resources. Our BSF (Black Soldier Fly) project turns organic waste into high-protein animal feed, creating a circular economy that benefits both the environment and local farmers.

🔥 Waste Disposal and Incineration

As part of our comprehensive waste management approach, Casper Waste Solutions operates certified incineration facilities for the safe destruction of non-recyclable and hazardous waste materials. Our advanced incineration technology ensures complete combustion while filtering harmful emissions, meeting all national environmental standards. This process significantly reduces landfill dependency and contributes to a cleaner, safer community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our waste management services.

What services does Casper Waste Solutions provide? ▼

We provide waste collection, recycling services, waste segregation, organic waste processing, hazardous waste disposal, and safe waste management solutions across Karatina and surrounding areas. Our services cover residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

Do you serve residential areas as well as businesses? ▼

Yes. We serve residential estates, apartments, offices, hotels, schools, and commercial institutions with reliable waste collection services in Karatina and beyond. No client is too small or too large for our team.

How often can you collect my waste? ▼

We offer flexible waste collection schedules, including daily, weekly pickups and on-demand services based on client needs. You can choose a plan that fits your household or business requirements.

Do you offer recycling services? ▼

Yes. We offer recycling services for plastics, paper, glass, and metals, helping reduce landfill waste and promote sustainability in Karatina. Our sorting facilities ensure maximum material recovery.

What happens to food and organic waste? ▼

We convert organic waste such as food and green waste into animal feed and organic fertilizer through sustainable recycling processes and our Black Soldier Fly (BSF) project.

What is the BSF project? ▼

Our BSF project is an innovative waste-to-resource system that transforms organic waste into high-protein animal feed and organic fertilizer using Black Soldier Fly larvae. It is an eco-friendly solution that reduces waste while creating valuable agricultural products.

How do I request your services? ▼

You can request our services by contacting us via phone/Whatsapp at +254 143 825 780, emailing casperwastesolutions427@gmail.com, clicking the "Request a Pickup" button, or filling out the contact form on our website.

Are your services affordable? ▼

Yes. We provide affordable and flexible waste management solutions tailored for homes, businesses, and institutions. Contact us for a free quote based on your specific needs.

How do you handle hazardous and non-recyclable waste? ▼

We safely dispose of medical waste, chemicals, and other hazardous materials through certified incineration and specialized disposal methods that comply with all environmental regulations. Safety and legal compliance are our top priorities.

Do you offer waste management training for businesses? ▼

Yes. We provide eco-friendly waste training and consulting services to help businesses implement proper waste handling, segregation, and recycling practices. Our team can conduct on-site workshops and create customized waste management plans.
